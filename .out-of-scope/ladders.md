# Ladders

GVK does not add climbable ladder blocks (for example Digi's Ladder mod or a port of its ladder logic).

## Why this is out of scope

Working ladders would significantly change how players design ships, bases and derelict interiors. Today vertical movement inside grids relies on the jetpack, elevators and stairs, and existing player builds, store prefabs and NPC/derelict prefabs are all designed around that. Introducing ladders mid-lifecycle would make those designs feel outdated and push a large wave of rebuilds, which is the kind of disruptive change the server avoids.

It also conflicts with the server's anti-mod-bloat rule: a new third-party mod dependency, with unknown multiplayer behavior on a dedicated server, for a quality-of-life feature that vanilla movement already covers.

The idea was deferred from Season 10 as a possible next-season change and then rejected for Season 11.
